Important NPC's

These are the most important NPC's in our story. They range from Lords to the poorest beggars of the world (when the party finally meets them...)

Important NPC's

Edric Vale

He is a politician the party met during their travels. He wants to ensure the city is safe and the laws are followed. If this safety is also granted to the lower classes of the city isn't really clear.

He has asked the party to find a box which was stolen from him by some criminals. The only thing the party knows is that the contents of the box is valuable to him. He is quite secretive about the real contents of the box, however.

Marice Holden

He is a reformist that strives for equality and is against corruption. He hasn't interacted much with the party, since his only encounter with party led to his death.
