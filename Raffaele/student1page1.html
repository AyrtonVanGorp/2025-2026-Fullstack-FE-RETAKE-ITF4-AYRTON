This page will explain more about important cities, demographics, tech and lots more. For now the party has only visited one city and this page will grow the more the story expands.

Built upon a foundation of ancient white stone and powered by the relentless flow of the Great River, The City stands as a monument to human and elven ingenuity. In this age of enlightenment, the clatter of horse-drawn carriages competes with the rhythmic hum of massive wooden waterwheels. These "Great Wheels" transfer power through a complex web of brass rods and oscillating pulleys, fueling the workshops of the world’s finest clockmakers and alchemists.

But for all its gilded beauty, The City is a place of harsh contrasts. Beneath the shadow of the Great Observatory lies a sprawling labyrinth of timber-framed tenements and narrow, fog-heavy alleys. In the upper districts, the elite study the stars and the arts; in the lower wards, the struggle for survival is the only science that matters.

The High Plaza

"Where the marble meets the sky and the gears never grind."

The crown jewel of The City. Home to the wealthy 10%, this district is built of white stone and polished brass. Here, the air is clean, and the streets are lit by alchemical lamps that never flicker.

  • Residents: Human Nobility & Elven Scholars
  • Security: High (Clockwork Sentinels)

The Low Ward

"Beneath the shadow of the great wheels, survival is the only law." A labyrinth of timber-framed tenements and narrow alleys. In The City's lower wards, the disenfranchised live among rusting pipes and creaking waterwheels. Crime is high, but so is the spirit of innovation.

  • Residents: Laborers, Artisans, and the Underworld
  • Security: Low (Guild-controlled)
