Roles

Dungeon Master

Xandro

Xandro serves as the Dungeon Master of our campaign. As the primary storyteller and world-builder, he is responsible for leading the game and meticulously preparing every session. From crafting intricate plotlines to controlling the NPCs, Xandro ensures that the world feels alive and that every player's choice has a meaningful impact on the journey.

Players

Ayrton

Ayrton plays the character Braum, a stout and spirited dwarf.

Though often referred to as 'Little Braum,' what he lacks in height, he more than makes up for in heart and appetite. Braum is a key member of the party, always ready with a pint in his hand or a weapon at his side.

While he might occasionally succumb to the temptations of a local tavern, his loyalty to his friends is as solid as the stone of his homeland. Ayrton brings a wonderful sense of character and humor to this sturdy adventurer.

Eufranio

Eufranio plays the character Dreyvonn, a formidable Orc Paladin.

Dreyvonn serves as the party’s shield and moral compass, blending the raw power of an Orc with the unwavering devotion of a Paladin. Clad in heavy armor and fueled by his oath, he stands as a beacon of protection for his companions.

Whether he is leading the charge into battle or offering aid to those in need, Eufranio’s portrayal of Dreyvonn brings a perfect balance of strength and righteousness to the group's journey.

Lyonn

Lyonn plays the character Liron, a cunning and lethal Drow Elf Rogue.

Liron is the shadows of the party, a Drow Elf who mastered the art of stealth and subterfuge long before reaching the surface. As a Rogue, he excels at navigating the unseen, whether he is scouting ahead or striking from the darkness with deadly precision.

Having traveled with Dreyvonn for a long time, Liron's sharp instincts and quick reflexes have saved the group more than once. Lyonn portrays Liron as a silent but vital ally who knows exactly when to stay hidden and when to strike.

Raffaele

Raffaele plays the character Silas, a charismatic and calculating Tiefling Warlock.

Silas is the arcane powerhouse of the group, a Tiefling whose silver tongue is just as dangerous as the eldritch powers he wields. Bound by a mysterious pact, he navigates the world with a sharp mind and a watchful eye, often remaining the only one thinking clearly when chaos erupts.

While his motives may sometimes be shrouded in shadow, his loyalty to the party is undeniable. Raffaele brings Silas to life as a master of both social intrigue and mystical combat, ensuring the group always has an edge over their enemies.
