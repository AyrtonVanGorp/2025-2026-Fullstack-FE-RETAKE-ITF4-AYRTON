Logs

Session: 1

The start of an adventure

The story begins when a dwarf named Braum arrives at a large manor. Invited by an important figure, he presents his invitation to the guard. Shortly after, an orc named Dreyvonn and a druid named Liron arrive. They have been traveling together for some time and heard that someone was seeking assistance. Even though they weren't officially invited, they are allowed inside. Moments later, a tiefling named Silas arrives, who—much like Braum—carries an invitation.

Inside, the four meet one another. The owner of the house, Edrick, calls them into a quiet room and gives them a task: retrieve an important chest from a warehouse. The group sets off immediately. Along the way, Braum thinks he spots a shadowy figure. When they reach the warehouse, they are ambushed by thugs, but they quickly defeat them. They find the chest, but upon opening it, it turns out to be empty. At that moment, a man named Merrick appears, only to be struck by a large arrow and killed instantly. Not long after, the guards arrive, and the four of them are arrested.

Session: 2

The Escape of the prison

In prison, they decide to make their escape. They climb out through a window, but Dreyvonn trips and breaks some glass, causing the guards to sound the alarm. The group runs for their lives and eventually manages to scale the gate. Later, they seek refuge in a bar. Braum drinks far too much and gets drunk, while Silas remains the only one thinking clearly.

Outside, Dreyvonn is suddenly pursued by other dwarves. In the ensuing fight, both he and Liron are knocked unconscious. When they regain consciousness, they find themselves in a place where Edrick's daughter is handing out soup to the poor. The scent of the soup is irresistible. Exhausted, they eat their fill and then all fall into a deep sleep, unaware of what awaits them the following day.
