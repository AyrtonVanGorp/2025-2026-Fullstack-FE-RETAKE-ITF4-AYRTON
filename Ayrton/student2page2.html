Backstory

Arannis Moonshadow grew up in an ancient elven city devoted to knowledge and arcane study. From a young age he showed a strong talent for magic and spent most of his life studying forgotten spells and magical artifacts.

Now he travels the world in search of lost knowledge, ancient ruins, and powerful magic that could change the fate of entire kingdoms.
